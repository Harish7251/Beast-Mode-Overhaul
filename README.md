## 🌒 The Story Behind This Project

Some players finish Dying Light: The Beast the honest way — scrap by scrap, chase by chase, one terrified sprint across a rooftop at 3 a.m. Others want to *study* the game. To dissect its movement systems, its stamina economy, its beast-mode timers, its fall damage curves — without dying forty times while doing it.

This companion suite was built for that second kind of player.

It is not a shortcut. It is a **magnifying glass**. A way to slow the chaos down, to bend the rules just enough that you can actually *see* how the machine works underneath. Think of it as a director's commentary track for a game that normally refuses to pause.

> "You don't tame the night. You just learn how to run through it with your eyes open."

This project has no connection to the game's publishers or developers. It is a fan-made, offline, single-player quality-of-life toolkit — shaped by the community, for the community, across the whole of 2026.

## ✨ Feature Overview

Here is where the toolkit earns its keep. Each feature was designed with a single guiding question: *does this make the game more readable, or just easier?* The answer, ideally, is both.

### 🩸 Endurance Without Limits
- Constant vitality, so a bad landing or a stray swipe doesn't end your exploration run.
- Stamina that refills the instant it drains — useful for parkour chain-testing.
- Sprint and climb without the usual gasping timer interrupting your flow.

### 🐺 Beast Mode, Extended
- Hold the transformation far longer than the base game intends, letting you observe every animation state.
- Adjustable duration scaling — from a small nudge to a full scene study session.
- No forced cooldown while the mode is active.

### 🪂 Gravity, Optional
- Fall damage suppressed entirely, or reduced to a configurable fraction.
- Ideal for mapping vertical routes across the city's tallest rooftops.
- Super jump to reach vantage points normally locked behind story progress.

### ⚙️ General Quality-of-Life
- Lightweight overlay with a clean, minimal footprint.
- Hotkeys rebindable from within the tool.
- Configuration saved between sessions, no re-tweaking every launch.
- Everything runs locally. No network calls, no telemetry, no accounts.

## 🖥️ Responsive Interface & Experience

The overlay was rebuilt from the ground up for 2026 with responsiveness as a first-class citizen, not an afterthought.

- **Adaptive scaling** — the panel reshapes itself to fit ultrawide monitors, 16:9 displays, and laptop screens alike.
- **Compact mode** — collapse the overlay into a slim strip when you only need one or two toggles visible.
- **Theme sync** — light and dark variants so the overlay never fights your desktop aesthetic.
- **Reduced motion option** — for players who prefer a calm interface over animated flourishes.
- **Keyboard-first navigation** — every control reachable without a mouse.

The result is an interface that stays out of the way until you need it, then gets out of the way again the second you don't.

## 📖 How It Fits Into Your Session

This is where the toolkit earns its place in your routine. Below is a walkthrough of the intended flow in 2026.

### Before You Launch
1. Make sure you are running the game in a single-player, offline context.
2. Confirm your configuration file is present (it ships with sensible defaults).
3. Adjust hotkeys if the defaults conflict with anything on your system.

### During Play
4. Bring up the overlay with your chosen hotkey.
5. Toggle individual enhancements on or off at will — nothing is permanent.
6. Watch the live status readouts to confirm each module is active.

### After Your Session
7. Disable everything from the overlay, or close the tool entirely.
8. Your settings persist automatically, ready for the next run.

No background services. No persistent hooks. Close it and it's gone.

## 🛠️ Frequently Asked Questions

**Does this require an internet connection?**
No. The entire toolkit operates offline. Nothing is phoned home, ever.

**Will this affect my saved games?**
It does not write to save files by default. Still, always keep a backup — good practice for any single-player tinkering.

**Can I turn individual features off?**
Yes. Every module has an independent toggle. Mix and match as you like.

**Is there a configuration file I can edit by hand?**
Yes, a plain text configuration file sits alongside the executable and is fully documented inside.

**What happens if the game updates?**
Most modules are resilient to minor patches. Major updates may require a companion update — check back periodically through 2026.

**Do you collect any data?**
Never. No analytics, no identifiers, no usage reports. The tool is silent by design.

## 🧪 Design Philosophy

Three principles guided every decision made while building this companion suite:

1. **Reversibility.** Every change is a toggle, not a commitment. Nothing should be permanent unless you ask it to be.
2. **Transparency.** The configuration is readable. The interface is honest about what is active. No hidden behavior.
3. **Respect for the original.** This toolkit exists to let players appreciate the game more deeply, not to replace it. The night is still scary — you've just been given a better flashlight.
